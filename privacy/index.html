Plain-language policy · effective 28 August 2026

Privacy

Parameter Playground keeps drafts in your browser. You can use every model without an account, analytics identifier, or server-side lesson database.

What stays on your device

Your lesson title, prompt, chart description, parameters, and seed are saved in local storage. This lets your draft survive a refresh.

Learner prediction and explanation text stays only on the current page. Clear this site’s browser data to erase a saved draft.

The sample demo uses a separate demo: storage key. Leaving the demo removes that sample key and never changes your regular draft.

What a shared link contains

A copied lesson link contains its settings directly in the URL. Anyone with the URL can read those settings.

Do not put student names, private class information, or sensitive information in lesson fields.

Network and offline use

The host receives ordinary request details such as IP address, time, and requested file in short-lived infrastructure logs.

The app adds no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts. A service worker caches the app for offline use.

Questions

Open an issue in the public source repository for privacy questions or deletion guidance.
